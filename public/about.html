Our Story

From Foundation to Legacy

Founded in 2009 by Er. Sham Vinnu, Suyog Constructions began with a singular vision — to build spaces that stand the test of time while exceeding client expectations. What started as a small contracting firm has grown into one of Pune's most respected construction companies.

Over 15 years, we have successfully delivered more than 500 projects spanning luxury residences, commercial complexes, renovations, and interior design — each one a testament to our unwavering commitment to quality, craftsmanship, and client satisfaction.

Our name — Suyog (meaning 'right opportunity' in Sanskrit) — reflects our philosophy: we help our clients seize the right opportunity to create something extraordinary.
